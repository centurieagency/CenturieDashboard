# Centurie Growth Academy: exam question bank

Rules: 25 questions drawn at random. Pass mark 80%, and **no critical error**. A question marked **CRITICAL** is failed if the candidate picks an answer marked ⛔ (a forbidden promise): this fails the whole exam, whatever the score. Retry after 24 hours.

The correct answer is marked ✅.
